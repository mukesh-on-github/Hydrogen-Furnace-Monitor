The Critical Challenge

Modern hydrogen furnaces lack an affordable, unified monitoring system.

  • Reliance on scattered, manual instruments.
  • Delayed analysis leads to poor control.
  • High safety risk with invisible H₂ leaks.

Our Solution

An integrated IoT-based Monitoring Ecosystem that connects directly to your hardware.

  • Live Streaming: H₂, Temp, Voltage & Power.
  • Universal: Works with ESP32 or CSV files.
  • Safe: Instant alarms for stability issues.
